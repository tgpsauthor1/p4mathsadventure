Digit Detectives

For the teacher

Compare from ten thousands to ones. Equal digits move the check one place right. The first different pair decides the comparison; smaller places are not checked after that. The pointed end faces the smaller number. Each question begins with an independent choice of > or <. Only an incorrect answer opens digit-by-digit remediation. After comparing the digits, the pupil retries the original symbol question without highlighting or answer cues. Three questions completed without errors in a row win. Any maths error resets the streak and marks the whole question as supported; corrections never earn stars. Fresh questions balance both symbol directions and vary the first differing place. No equal whole-number pairs are generated. No information is stored or sent. Listen uses available device voices.
