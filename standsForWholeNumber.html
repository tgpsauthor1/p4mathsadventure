Digit Quest: Stands For

Take your time.
For the teacher

Accept numeric amounts and equivalent counted-place phrases (e.g. 80000 or 8 ten thousands). A place name alone is not a complete answer. Five-digit whole numbers; non-zero target digits occur once. Positions are shuffled in cycles. A win requires three consecutive independent answers. Any mathematical error in the challenge resets the streak. Guided corrections, support practice and the original-question retry never count towards winning. After an error, the correct answer is explained. The explanation is then hidden and the child retries. For a wrong full answer, the child revisits the digit, place and value before retrying the full question. Blank or malformed numeric entries receive a formatting prompt without losing progress. Nothing is sent or stored. Read aloud uses voices available on this device.
